Where to Mail Your Credit Dispute Letter

Send your FCRA dispute letter to the bureau that's reporting the error. Use Certified Mail with Return Receipt and enclose copies of your supporting documents.

Always verify before mailing. Bureaus occasionally update their P.O. boxes. The address printed on your own credit report's dispute instructions always takes priority over any third-party list, including this one.

Tips for mailing your dispute

  • Send a separate letter to each bureau reporting the error.
  • Use USPS Certified Mail with Return Receipt for dated proof of delivery.
  • Enclose copies — never originals — of your evidence.
  • Keep a copy of the signed letter and your mailing receipt.
  • Include your full name, current address, and last 4 of your SSN so the bureau can match your file.
