Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

FCRADisputeHelper.com ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is and isn't collected when you use this website.

Information you enter into the generator stays in your browser

Our dispute letter generator runs entirely on your device using client-side JavaScript. The name, address, Social Security digits, account numbers, and any other details you type are used only to build your letter in your browser. This information is never transmitted to us, stored on a server, or shared with any third party. When you close the tab, it is gone.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics may log standard technical data such as your browser type, device, approximate region, and pages visited. This is aggregate, non-identifying information used to keep the site running and understand general usage.

Advertising and cookies

We display ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or device identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Users may opt out of the use of these cookies through the links above.

Third-party links

This site links to external resources such as AnnualCreditReport.com, the credit bureaus, and the CFPB. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect or store personal information you enter into the generator, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. For data handled by our advertising and analytics partners, you may exercise rights under the GDPR or CCPA directly through those providers' privacy controls linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@fcradisputehelper.com.
